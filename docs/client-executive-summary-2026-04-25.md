## What We Built This Week

### 1. Mobile-First Redesign
The site was rebuilt from the ground up for phone users. Every page now looks and works the way a homeowner would expect on their phone — fast load times, large tap targets, no content getting cut off, and no awkward scrolling. The homepage hero, all service pages, and the quote page were each individually optimized.

### 2. Your Giraffe Mascot — Fully Custom
We replaced all generic Owens Corning and Pink Panther branding with your custom SC Roofing giraffe. The mascot now appears in two places:
- **Bottom-left corner of the homepage** — tapping it opens Text, Chat, or Book options
- **Right edge of every page** — a "peek-in" giraffe tab that opens the instant roof quote tool

Both mascots wear the blue hard hat and SC Roofing uniform.

### 3. New "Get Started" Button — 3 Clear Paths
The old "Free Estimate" button is gone. In its place is a "Get Started — No Obligations" button that gives visitors three choices:
- **Text us** — opens a pre-filled text message on mobile; copies the number on desktop
- **Have our team contact you** — a short form that goes straight to your inbox
- **Get an instant quote** — launches the Roofle roof measurement tool immediately

All three options are available in English and Spanish.

### 4. Legal Compliance for SMS Marketing
Before we can send any text messages to customers, two things are required by law: a Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Both are now live on the website. Every text/SMS button on the site requires the customer to check a consent box before their number is captured. This is required for 10DLC carrier registration (covered below).

### 5. Roof Quote Page — Full Rebuild
The `/roof-quote` page is now a full educational landing experience, not just a tool. It includes:
- A clearer, more readable hero section
- Your correct shingle tiers in order: Oakridge (Good) → Duration (Better, 130 mph) → TAMKO Storm Fighter (Best, 160 mph)
- Three custom-designed infographics explaining why quotes vary, what a real quote includes, and what drives roof cost in South Carolina
- A bilingual Roofle instant quote tool with six entry points throughout the page
- Your AI chat assistant available directly on the page

### 6. Bilingual Spanish Toggle
The entire site now has a Spanish/English toggle in the header. Homeowners can switch to Spanish at any time and every section — the navigation, CTAs, quote page, financing calculator, About page, and all service area pages — displays in Spanish. This was built with a full translation system, not a third-party plugin.

## What's Next

The website is ready to go live. Two things need to happen on the integration side, and then we connect your domain.

### Zuper CRM Connection
We have a placeholder in place on the site where the Zuper scheduling button lives. Once we have your Zuper account details and webhook, we wire it in — the button goes from "Coming Soon" to a live booking experience. This is a short task once we have access.

### 10DLC SMS Carrier Registration
Before you can text customers through any platform (Zuper, Twilio, or otherwise), the major carriers (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile) require your business to register. This is called 10DLC. The process takes 1–3 weeks once submitted. We have everything in place to submit — Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and opt-in language on every CTA. We just need your confirmation on which SMS platform you're going with (Zuper native, or a separate service like Twilio) and we file immediately.

### Domain Cutover
When you're ready, we connect `restorationroofingsc.com` to the new site, run a final quality check, and go live. We have access to your GoDaddy account to handle the DNS side.
