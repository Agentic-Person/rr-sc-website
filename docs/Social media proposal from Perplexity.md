# OK, so using your guidelines and playing by the rules I would like to plan to set up an automated agent for his Facebook page that makes 1 to 3 posts daily Monday through Friday again using your guidelines guard rails and the official channels for Meta but then I’d also like to push the same post to Instagram and maybe we’re not posting one to three every day about posting something every day on both those two social media channels what would a what would somebody pay for this or what what is the cost associated? I don’t even know where to say it what would the social media Director or somebody charge for such?

Yes — for a compliant setup where one system creates a daily post and publishes it to both the client’s Facebook Page and Instagram through Meta Business Suite or official Meta-connected workflows, you can absolutely package this as a recurring service, and the market usually prices it as a monthly retainer rather than “per automation.”[^1][^2][^3][^4]

For a small business using Facebook + Instagram together, a realistic range is usually about **\$750 to \$2,500 per month** for basic-to-strong ongoing management, with higher pricing when strategy, community management, approvals, reporting, and paid ads are included.[^5][^6][^7][^8]

## What buyers pay for

What the client is really paying for is not just “posts,” but a bundle of work:

- Content planning, writing, scheduling, and publishing across Facebook and Instagram.[^2][^1]
- Monitoring comments and messages, even if AI handles first-pass triage.[^6][^5]
- Strategy, guard rails, revisions, analytics, and platform management.[^8][^1]
- In your case, they are also paying for a custom automation system using official channels, which is more valuable than basic scheduler-only service.[^3][^9]

That matters because market pricing for “12 posts/month” is often lower, while pricing climbs once you add approvals, automation oversight, and ad support.[^7][^10][^5]

## Practical pricing bands

Here’s a clean way to think about pricing for your specific offer:


| Service level | Typical monthly price | What it usually includes |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| Basic posting package | \$500–\$1,000/mo [^2][^10] | 8–20 posts/month, light scheduling, basic graphics, little strategy. |
| Standard management | \$1,000–\$2,000/mo [^1][^6][^10] | Facebook + Instagram, ongoing posting, caption writing, moderate engagement, reporting. |
| Premium / specialist | \$2,000–\$3,500+/mo [^2][^8][^10] | Strategy, community management, approvals, analytics, custom workflows, and some paid social coordination. |
| Ads management add-on | \$650–\$6,000/mo, often separate from ad spend [^2] | Campaign setup, optimization, reporting, policy review, but usually not media spend itself. |

For your roofing client, I would not position this as “cheap social posting.” I’d position it as **AI-assisted social media operations** for a local service business, because it includes automation design, compliance guard rails, and operational oversight beyond normal social posting.[^9][^1]

## What I’d charge

For the exact setup you described — one post per weekday pushed to both Facebook and Instagram, guard rails, official-channel publishing, comment triage, and optional ads workflow with human approval — I’d frame it in **three parts**:

- **Setup fee:** \$1,500–\$5,000 one time.[^1][^2]
This covers Meta setup, linking Facebook + Instagram, automation design, prompts, approval logic, escalation rules, and testing. The market sources above focus mostly on monthly management, but custom automation architecture is separate implementation work and should not be bundled in for free.[^2][^8][^1]
- **Monthly management retainer:** \$1,250–\$2,500/mo.[^6][^7][^1]
This is the sweet spot for a small local business needing daily presence on two channels, with AI-assisted content generation and light community management. The higher end makes more sense when you are reviewing outputs, handling exceptions, and maintaining the automation.[^10][^1]
- **Paid ads management add-on:** \$500–\$1,500/mo plus ad spend.[^7][^2]
Since you want human approval before activation, this becomes an operations layer on top of campaign creation and review. If you actively optimize campaigns weekly, the fee should trend higher.[^11][^2]


## A strong offer structure

I’d package it like this for a roofing company:

- **Starter:** \$1,250/mo
    - 20–22 posts/month, published to Facebook + Instagram.[^10][^6]
    - AI-generated drafts with human-reviewed guard rails.
    - Basic comment monitoring and monthly reporting.
- **Growth:** \$1,750/mo
    - Daily weekday posting to both channels.[^4][^3]
    - Comment triage and reply suggestions.
    - Monthly strategy refresh, promotions, storm-event content, and reputation monitoring.
- **Authority:** \$2,500/mo
    - Everything in Growth.
    - Priority edits, deeper analytics, campaign coordination, seasonal roofing promos, and ads workflow with human approval.[^8][^2]

For a roofing niche client, this is easier to justify when tied to lead generation themes like inspections, storm response, insurance education, before/after jobs, crew credibility, and local trust content rather than “brand awareness” alone.

## How to say it

The cleanest language is probably:

- “AI-assisted social media management for Facebook and Instagram.”
- “Automated daily posting with human-supervised guard rails.”
- “Managed Meta content operations.”
- “Social media director service with automation backbone.”

I would avoid saying “fully autonomous social media bot,” even if that is partly true under the hood, because clients hear “unattended risk,” while “AI-assisted management with human oversight” sounds safer and more professional.[^9][^11]

## My recommendation

For this client, I’d probably quote:

- **\$2,500–\$4,000 one-time setup**, depending on how much custom agent work is involved.[^1][^2][^8]
- **\$1,500–\$2,000/month ongoing** for weekday posting to Facebook + Instagram plus comment triage and reporting.[^6][^10][^1]
- **Add \$750–\$1,250/month** if you are also managing Meta ad workflows with approvals and optimization.[^2][^7]

That pricing is high enough to reflect the real value, still aligned with broader social media management ranges for small businesses, and more defensible because you are delivering a system, not just writing captions.[^1][^2][^6]
