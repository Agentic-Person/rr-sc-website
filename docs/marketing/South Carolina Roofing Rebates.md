## Section 1: The Opportunity

### Your Roof May Do More Than Protect Your Home — It May Qualify for Upgrade Assistance

South Carolina coastal homeowners face a unique combination of heat, humidity, salt air, wind-driven rain, tropical storms, and hurricane exposure. Because of that risk, South Carolina has programs designed to help homeowners strengthen their homes against severe weather.

For many homeowners in Charleston and surrounding coastal areas, the most relevant program is the **SC Safe Home Mitigation Grant Program**. This program is designed to help eligible homeowners complete approved mitigation upgrades, including certain roof retrofit improvements that reduce wind and water intrusion risk.

### Key Message
This is not a basic roof replacement rebate. It is a storm-hardening and mitigation program for approved improvements.

---

## Section 2: What Is the SC Safe Home Program?

The SC Safe Home Mitigation Grant Program is administered by the South Carolina Department of Insurance. It provides grant funding for qualifying homeowners in designated coastal counties who want to make their homes stronger against hurricanes and high winds.

### Program Intent
The program helps eligible homeowners upgrade vulnerable parts of the home envelope, especially the roof and exterior openings.

### Examples of Qualifying Roof-Related Improvements

- Strengthening roof deck attachment
- Installing a secondary water barrier to reduce water intrusion
- Upgrading to high wind-rated roof covering
- Bracing gable ends
- Reinforcing roof-to-wall connections
- Strengthening weak trusses, studs, or other structural components

### Potential Grant Amounts
According to the South Carolina Department of Insurance, grant award amounts vary by project type, project cost, household income, family size, and local/state income guidelines. Published award categories include:

| Project Type | Non-Matching Grant | Matching Grant |
|---|---:|---:|
| Resilient Mitigation | Up to $7,500 | Up to $6,000 |
| Sustainable Mitigation | Up to $5,000 | Up to $4,000 |
| Hurricane Shutters / Protective Barriers | Up to $3,000 | Up to $3,000 |

**Plain-English positioning:** qualified homeowners may be able to reduce out-of-pocket costs when approved roof upgrades are part of a storm-resilience improvement plan.

## Section 3: Who May Qualify?

Eligibility depends on current SC Safe Home rules and available funding windows. Generally, the program is intended for homeowners who:

- Live in a designated South Carolina coastal county
- Own and occupy the home as their primary residence
- Have a single-family, freestanding home
- Maintain an active homeowner’s insurance policy
- Have not already been awarded and used a Safe Home grant
- Have no prior storm-related damage or insurance claim payouts on the home

### Important Disclaimer
Restoration Roofing SC is not the grant administrator and cannot guarantee approval, eligibility, or award amount. The homeowner must apply through the official SC Safe Home process.

## Section 7: FAQ

### Is this a free roof program?
No. The SC Safe Home program is not a free roof program. It is a mitigation grant program for approved storm-hardening improvements. Some qualified homeowners may receive financial assistance, but approval and award amounts vary.

### Can I use it for a normal roof replacement?
Not by itself. The program is not designed to pay for ordinary repairs, remodeling, or basic roof replacement. It is focused on approved safety and storm-resilience upgrades.

### Does Charleston County qualify?
Charleston is a coastal South Carolina market, and the SC Safe Home program is designed for designated coastal counties. Homeowners should verify eligibility through the official SC Safe Home portal.

### Can Restoration Roofing SC apply for me?
The homeowner is responsible for applying through the official process. Restoration Roofing SC can help explain roofing options and provide documentation or estimates where appropriate.

### What should I do first?
Start with a professional roof inspection and storm-readiness review. That gives you a clearer understanding of your roof condition and whether upgrade planning makes sense.
