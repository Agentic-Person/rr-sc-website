Your Roof May Do More Than Protect Your Home

In coastal South Carolina, your roof is a major part of your storm preparation plan. Programs like SC Safe Home are designed to help eligible homeowners strengthen their homes against hurricanes, wind-driven rain, and water intrusion.

This is not a basic roof replacement rebate. It is a storm-hardening and mitigation pathway for approved improvements.

What Is the SC Safe Home Program?

The SC Safe Home Mitigation Grant Program is administered by the South Carolina Department of Insurance. It helps eligible coastal homeowners make approved safety upgrades such as stronger roofs, storm shutters, and other improvements that reduce wind and water intrusion.

Roof Deck Attachment

Helps roof panels stay secure during high wind events.

Secondary Water Barrier

Adds protection against water intrusion if shingles are compromised.

High Wind-Rated Covering

Upgrades the outer roof surface for stronger storm performance.

Potential Grant Amounts

Award amounts vary by project type, project cost, household income, family size, and current program rules. Published SC Safe Home categories include:

Who May Qualify?

Eligibility depends on active SC Safe Home rules and funding windows. Generally, the program is intended for designated coastal county homeowners who own and occupy a single-family, freestanding primary residence and maintain active homeowner’s insurance.

Important Homeowner Note

Restoration Roofing SC is not the grant administrator and cannot guarantee approval, eligibility, or award amount. Homeowners must apply through the official SC Safe Home process.

Common Questions

Is this a free roof program?

No. SC Safe Home is a mitigation grant program for approved storm-hardening improvements. Some qualified homeowners may receive financial assistance, but approval and award amounts vary.

Can I use it for a normal roof replacement?

Not by itself. The program is not designed to pay for ordinary repairs, remodeling, or basic roof replacement. It is focused on approved safety and storm-resilience upgrades.

Does Charleston County qualify?

Charleston is a coastal South Carolina market, and the program is designed for designated coastal counties. Homeowners should verify eligibility through the official SC Safe Home portal.

Can Restoration Roofing SC apply for me?

The homeowner is responsible for applying through the official process. Restoration Roofing SC can explain roofing options and provide estimates or documentation where appropriate.
